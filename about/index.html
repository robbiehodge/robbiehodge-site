Differentiators

What makes Robbie Hodge different.

Founder, agency, and in-house — all three.

Most consultants come from one world. Robbie has operated across all three — the scrappy resourcefulness of a founder, the breadth and speed of an agency, and the accountability and depth of an in-house leader. That combination is rare and it shows in how fast he can move.

He knows the full playbook and can deploy it.

From brand positioning to performance marketing, content strategy to revenue attribution — Robbie doesn't hand things off to junior staff or subcontract the hard parts. He builds the full-funnel system himself and then teaches the team to run it.

Proven at scale.

As SVP of Marketing at Varda Partners, Robbie led the strategy behind a YouTube-powered DTC brand to 1.6 million subscribers, #1 category ranking, and nine figures in attributable revenue. This is not theory. This is a repeatable playbook that has worked at scale.

Charlotte-based with national reach.

Most fractional CMOs are remote-only. Robbie is based in Charlotte, NC and available for in-person strategy sessions, team meetings, and board presentations across the Charlotte metro. For local businesses, that matters.

Revenue is the only metric that matters.

Impressions, followers, and traffic are inputs. Revenue and pipeline are outputs. Every engagement Robbie takes is structured around real business outcomes — not vanity metrics, not activity reports, not dashboards full of numbers that don't connect to money.

Background

The person behind the work.

Robbie Hodge spent 18+ years building marketing engines for eCommerce and DTC brands before launching his consulting practice in 2025. His career spans founder-stage companies, agency work, and senior in-house leadership — giving him a rare end-to-end view of how marketing actually works at every stage of growth.

Most recently as SVP of Marketing at Varda Partners, Robbie led the marketing strategy behind a YouTube-powered DTC powerhouse in the specialty retail space — scaling it to 1.6 million subscribers, the #1 position in its category, and nine figures in attributable revenue. Before that, years of building marketing functions across consumer products, media, and B2B organizations from startup through enterprise.

He is based in Charlotte, NC, where he also serves on the Plaza Midwood Neighborhood Association board as digital communications lead. Outside of client work, Robbie owns and operates Hodge Industries, a premium accordion and folding door business serving the Charlotte market.

He loves scale. He loves a challenge. Most of all, he loves solutions.
