Common Questions

Fractional CMO
Charlotte FAQ.

What does a fractional CMO do for a Charlotte business?

A fractional CMO provides senior marketing leadership on a part-time or project basis — the same strategic capability as a full-time CMO at a fraction of the cost. For Charlotte businesses, that means brand strategy, demand generation, team building, performance marketing, and go-to-market execution without the $250K+ full-time overhead.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Charlotte?

Fractional CMO engagements in Charlotte typically run $5,000–$15,000 per month depending on scope and hours. This compares favorably to a full-time CMO at $200,000–$350,000 per year including benefits and equity. Most Charlotte businesses see meaningful ROI within the first 90 days.

What industries do you serve in Charlotte?

My core expertise is eCommerce, DTC brands, and B2B companies with complex lead generation needs. In the Charlotte market I work with specialty retailers, consumer brands, and B2B companies looking to build or scale their marketing function without a full-time executive hire.

Are you available for in-person meetings in Charlotte?

Yes — I am based in Charlotte, NC and available for in-person strategy sessions, team meetings, and board presentations across the Charlotte metro. I also work with brands nationwide on a remote basis.

How quickly can a fractional CMO engagement start?

Most engagements can begin within two weeks of initial conversations. The first 30 days typically focus on a marketing audit, competitive analysis, and building a prioritized 90-day roadmap with clear milestones.

How is this different from hiring a Charlotte marketing agency?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing executive, not an agency. I provide strategic leadership, accountability, and decision-making authority — not deliverables. I can work alongside your existing agencies, manage them on your behalf, or help you build an in-house team.
